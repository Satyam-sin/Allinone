01 Introduction

What is Trigonometry?

Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies the relationships between the sides and angles of triangles, especially right-angled triangles. It is widely used in engineering, navigation, physics, astronomy, surveying, and architecture.

02 Ratios

Basic Trigonometric Ratios

In a right triangle, with respect to angle θ, the three sides are: Perpendicular, Base, and Hypotenuse.

sin θ \[ \sin\theta=\frac{\text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} \]
cos θ \[ \cos\theta=\frac{\text{Base}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} \]
tan θ \[ \tan\theta=\frac{\text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Base}} =\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} \]
Reciprocal Ratios \[ \csc\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}, \quad \sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}, \quad \cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta} \]

04 Quadrants

Quadrant Rules (ASTC Rule)

In each quadrant, only certain trigonometric ratios are positive. The mnemonic "All Students Take Calculus" (ASTC) helps remember this rule.

Quadrant Angle Range Positive Ratios
I 0°–90° All
II 90°–180° sin, cosec
III 180°–270° tan, cot
IV 270°–360° cos, sec

05 Identities

Basic Trigonometric Identities

Pythagorean Identity 1 \[ \sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1 \]
Pythagorean Identity 2 \[ 1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta \]
Pythagorean Identity 3 \[ 1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta \]
06 Formulae

Sum & Difference Formulae

sin(A ± B) \[ \sin(A\pm B)=\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B \]
cos(A ± B) \[ \cos(A\pm B)=\cos A\cos B\mp\sin A\sin B \]
tan(A ± B) \[ \tan(A\pm B)= \frac{\tan A\pm\tan B} {1\mp\tan A\tan B} \]
07 Formulae

Double & Triple Angle Formulae

sin 2A \[ \sin2A=2\sin A\cos A \]
cos 2A \[ \cos2A=\cos^2A-\sin^2A=1-2\sin^2A \]
tan 2A \[ \tan2A=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A} \]
sin 3A \[ \sin3A=3\sin A-4\sin^3A \]
cos 3A \[ \cos3A=4\cos^3A-3\cos A \]
08 Transformations

Sum-to-Product Formulas

sin C + sin D \[ 2\sin\!\left(\tfrac{C+D}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\tfrac{C-D}{2}\right) \]
sin C − sin D \[ 2\cos\!\left(\tfrac{C+D}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\tfrac{C-D}{2}\right) \]
cos C + cos D \[ 2\cos\!\left(\tfrac{C+D}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\tfrac{C-D}{2}\right) \]
cos C − cos D \[ -2\sin\!\left(\tfrac{C+D}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\tfrac{C-D}{2}\right) \]
09 Inverse Functions

Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Inverse trigonometric functions determine the angle corresponding to a given trigonometric ratio.

Function Domain Principal Range
\(\sin^{-1}x\) [−1, 1] [−90°, 90°]
\(\cos^{-1}x\) [−1, 1] [0°, 180°]
\(\tan^{-1}x\) All Real Numbers (−90°, 90°)

13 Practice

Solved Examples

Example 1
If \(\sin\theta=\frac35\), find \(\cos\theta\) and \(\tan\theta\).

Solution: Using the 3-4-5 right triangle, Hypotenuse = 5, Opposite = 3, Adjacent = 4.

\(\cos\theta=\frac45,\qquad\tan\theta=\frac34\)
Example 2
Evaluate: \(\sin30^\circ\cos60^\circ+\cos30^\circ\sin60^\circ\)

Solution: Apply the identity \(\sin(A+B)\), where A = 30° and B = 60°.

Value = 1
Example 3
A person stands 50 m away from a tower. The angle of elevation to the top of the tower is 30°. Find the height of the tower.

Solution: \(\tan30^\circ=\frac{h}{50}\Rightarrow h=50\times\frac1{\sqrt3}\)

Height ≈ 28.87 m
Example 4
Prove: \(\frac{1-\cos^2\theta}{\sin\theta}=\sin\theta\)

Solution: Since \(1-\cos^2\theta=\sin^2\theta\), therefore \(\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\sin\theta}=\sin\theta\).

Hence Proved.
